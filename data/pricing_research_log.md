
# Pricing Research Log

## 1. Research objective

Track published API pricing for selected AI models over time and evaluate how pricing changes affect the cost of AI-assisted business workflows.

## 2. Research questions

- How have published input and output token prices changed over time?
- How do prices compare across selected AI providers?
- How much does a model's token price affect total task cost?
- How do human review, output quality and rework change the business case?

## 3. Evidence standards

- Prioritise official provider documentation and dated announcements.
- Record input, output and cached-input prices separately.
- Distinguish API pricing from consumer subscriptions.
- Record publication dates separately from effective dates.
- Link each pricing observation to its supporting source.
- Never invent missing historical prices.
- Label estimates, assumptions and experimental measurements distinctly.

## 4. Provider scope

Initial candidates:
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google
- DeepSeek

Include a provider only when relevant pricing evidence can be verified.

## 5. Research workflow

1. Identify eligible models and pricing tiers.
2. Locate official and dated evidence.
3. Record sources in the source register.
4. Extract and standardise pricing observations.
5. Validate dates, units and calculations.
6. Document evidence gaps and comparability limitations.

## 6. Open decisions

- Which models have sufficient historical evidence?
- Which historical periods can be compared fairly?
- Which business task should be used for the controlled experiment?
- What quality criteria will determine an accepted output?
