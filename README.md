# Enterprise AI Value Tracker

## Project Overview

The Enterprise AI Value Tracker is an independent business analysis project designed to evaluate the economics and business value of artificial intelligence.

The project investigates how AI model pricing changes over time and whether AI-assisted workflows can reduce task costs, improve productivity, and deliver measurable business value compared with traditional manual processes.

The analysis focuses on transparent calculations, reproducible methods, reliable sources, and evidence-based business recommendations.

## Research Questions

1. How have published input and output token prices for selected AI models changed over time?
2. How do AI usage costs vary across providers, models, and workload sizes?
3. How does AI-assisted task completion compare with manual work in terms of time, cost, and quality?
4. Under what conditions does AI produce a positive return on investment?
5. What factors beyond token prices influence the economic value of AI adoption?

## Project Scope

### Workstream 1: AI Pricing Economics
- Collect published AI model pricing data.
- Record effective dates, model names, pricing units, and source URLs.
- Analyse historical price changes where reliable evidence is available.
- Compare input, output, and applicable additional usage costs.

### Workstream 2: Productivity and Task Economics
- Define a repeatable business task.
- Compare manual and AI-assisted workflows.
- Measure task completion time, review effort, quality, and rework.
- Estimate cost per successfully completed task.

### Workstream 3: Business Value and ROI
- Develop a transparent cost-benefit model.
- Estimate break-even points and potential savings.
- Test different usage volumes and labour-cost assumptions.
- Document limitations, risks, and business recommendations.

## Research Integrity

Published facts, measured results, assumptions, estimates, and forecasts will be clearly distinguished.

Historical pricing claims will be supported by dated evidence wherever possible. Missing data will not be invented. Experimental findings will be reported only when the relevant tests have actually been conducted.

AI cost estimates will distinguish usage-based API charges from fixed subscription costs and will account for human review and quality where measurable.

## Limitations

This is an independent analytical project, not an official assessment endorsed by any AI provider. Results will depend on the selected models, tasks, data, assumptions, and observation period.

Conclusions will be limited to the evidence collected and will not automatically generalise to every enterprise workflow.
